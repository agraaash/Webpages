Retail Channel Distribution Breakdown

While quick-commerce and modern trade grow in cities, 80% of FMCG revenues remain anchored in general trade kirana stores due to rural penetration.

Distribution Mechanics: Numeric vs. Weighted Distribution

Example Scenario: 2 Outlets in Locality Category Volume: 100 Units
Store A (High Demand) Sells 75 units (75%) ITC Biscuit Available
Store B (Low Demand) Sells 25 units (25%) Britannia Available

• Numeric Distribution: Both brands have 50% numeric reach (1 of 2 stores).

• Weighted Distribution: ITC reaches 75% of demand, while Britannia reaches 25%.

Takeaway: High weighted distribution ensures presence where volume actually moves.
